## Main Areas

Exactly six:

- **Health:** strong, fresh and physically alive woodland; moving water, rocks, grasses, ferns, elevation and bright natural light.
- **Mind:** original contemplative ancient woodland inspired by the atmosphere of Okunoin/Koyasan without reproducing it; towering vertical trees, deep shade, mossy weathered stone, narrow winding paths, filtered light, low mist and muted greens. No cemetery elements, statues, sacred structures or identifiable architecture.
- **Self:** free, curious and expressive; varied terrain, unusual vegetation, wildflowers, clearings and winding paths.
- **People:** warm, open and connected; shared clearings, flowering vegetation, interconnected paths, grouped trees and warmer light.
- **Work:** focused and expansive; higher ground, strong sightlines, rocks, defined natural paths and distant views.
- **Wealth:** stable, mature and enduring; deep terrain, stone, visible roots, slow water, moss and sheltered mature woodland. Avoid overt gold/luxury imagery.

## Special environments

### Nursery

First environment; protected, warm, fertile, optimistic and peaceful. Woodland clearing, filtered warm light, young grasses, moss, stones, tiny spring, mature woodland and paths deeper into the Forest. Contains only Seed, Sprout and Young Plant.

### Resting Grove

Intentional pause, never failure. Permanent pre-dawn atmosphere with blue-grey light, dense ancient woodland, heavy low mist, dew, damp ground, dark water and restrained movement. Mysterious but never threatening. Trees remain healthy.

### Completed Grove

One shared mature grove for completed Goal Trees. At approximately three completed Trees from an Area, that region begins acquiring the Area's character; at five or more the Area identity is clearly established. Area echoes blend organically rather than becoming six rooms. Meaning: legacy.

### Arboretum

Hidden elevated botanical valley for Achievement Trees: terraces, rock, flowing water, long views, exceptional natural light and generous spacing. It must never resemble a trophy cabinet. No empty pedestals, grey locked Trees or missing-item checklist. It is discovered with the first Achievement Tree.

## Shared environment kit

Future reusable, layered assets include:

- Water: spring, pools, streams, flow, cascades, waterfall
- Geology: rocks, stones, boulders, moss-covered rock, exposed earth
- Vegetation: moss, lichen, ferns, grasses, undergrowth, wildflowers, plants, mushrooms, fungi
- Woodland: roots, fallen branches, logs, stumps, debris
- Navigation: natural trails and appropriate stone paths
- Ground: soil, leaves, woodland floor, wet ground, cover
- Atmosphere: mist, fog, light shafts, restrained wind, subtle leaf movement and environmental depth

Forest V1 must be layered and composable, not a set of static flattened backgrounds.

## Future asset architecture

```text
Supabase Storage / CDN
→ Fovyn Asset Service
→ versioned manifests
→ client renderer
→ device cache
```

Heavy production imagery belongs in object storage/CDN, not GitHub. GitHub may contain manifests, stable IDs, configuration, mappings, metadata, versions and tiny development placeholders.

Persistent Goal/Tree data must reference stable identities rather than filenames. Initial identity examples:

- `tree.growth.common_juniper`

## Future functional acceptance journey

```text
Create Goal
→ Seed appears in Nursery
→ qualifying real-world record contributes
→ Sprout
→ Young Plant
→ Common Juniper graduates to the correct Area
→ canonical Growth ladder advances
→ Tree health reflects current Goal performance
→ Goal rests and Tree enters Resting Grove
→ Goal resumes and Tree returns to its Area
→ Goal completes and Tree enters Completed Grove
→ Completed Grove evolves over time
```

Achievement conditions reveal Achievement Trees in the Arboretum independently. Legendary Trees remain long-term discoveries.

Forest progression must always derive from the real Tracking and Goal engines. It must never become a separate progression or game system.
